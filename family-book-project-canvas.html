Project purpose

Choose one family to examine throughout the project: your own family or a family you know. Apply sociology to explain how culture, institutions, inequality, policy, technology, historical change, and the life course influence that family. Protect privacy by changing names and omitting sensitive or identifying details.

What every section slide requires

  • A focused title and approximately 100–150 words written by you
  • At least two accurately applied course concepts in bold
  • A connection to a social institution, policy, culture, technology, or historical pattern
  • One meaningful image representing the slide’s main idea; it may be a personal photo, properly sourced image, chart, diagram, or AI-generated image
  • Alternative text for the image and a source citation or AI-generated-image label
  • Citations for outside facts, quotations, statistics, cases, and images

Complete the numbered slide during the module that covers the same numbered coursebook section. Each coursebook section ends with suggestions, a model slide plan, and a link to a planning coach.

AI use for this project

Permitted: AI may explain a difficult concept, check grammar after you have written your content, or help create/edit an image.

Not permitted: AI may not write, rewrite, paraphrase, expand, summarize, or generate your slide content, examples, analysis, reflections, takeaways, citations, or speaker notes. Every sentence submitted as project content must be written by you.

Verify all facts and citations. Label AI-created visuals as AI-generated illustration and follow your instructor’s citation directions. Do not create deceptive images of real people or events.

Accessibility, accommodations, and privacy

The coursebook’s interactive activities are optional, ungraded practice. They are not required to earn points or demonstrate completion, and the same core concepts appear in text, descriptions, tables, and feedback. A student may skip an interaction or request an equivalent text-based activity without a grade penalty.

If any page, image, chart, video, download, or activity creates a disability-related barrier, contact the instructor and the college Disability Services/Accessibility Office. An accessible alternative or reasonable accommodation will be provided as promptly as possible and without penalty.

Do not enter names, diagnoses, family secrets, or other sensitive information in public practice tools. Submit graded work only through Canvas or another institutionally approved system. Protect the privacy of the family used in the project by changing names and omitting identifying details.

Accessibility, Disability Accommodation, Practice Activities, and Privacy

This page may be linked from the syllabus or copied into Canvas. Last reviewed September 2026.

Accessibility commitment

This digital coursebook was designed and reviewed using the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and ADA Title II accessibility principles. It uses semantic headings, keyboard-operable native controls, visible keyboard focus, alternative text for instructional images, labeled form controls, readable sans-serif type, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support where animation appears.

Important: Accessibility is an ongoing process. No automated scan or editorial review can guarantee access for every person, device, browser, or assistive technology. Students are encouraged to report barriers so they can be corrected.

Disability access and reasonable accommodations

If any coursebook page, image, chart, video, downloadable file, or activity creates a barrier, contact the instructor and the college Disability Services/Accessibility Office using the contact information in the official syllabus or college website. An accessible alternative or reasonable accommodation will be provided as promptly as possible and without penalty.

Students are not required to disclose a diagnosis publicly or to classmates. Formal accommodation requests should follow the college’s established process; immediate content barriers may also be reported directly to the instructor.

Interactive content is optional practice

All coursebook sliders, quizzes, inventories, animations, planners, and other interactive activities are optional, ungraded practice. They are not required to earn points or demonstrate completion.

The same core ideas are provided through text, tables, descriptions, explanations, and feedback. Students may skip an interaction or request an equivalent text-based activity without a grade penalty. Interactive results are not diagnostic and should not be used to label oneself or another person.

Privacy and sensitive information

  • The public coursebook does not ask students to create an account or submit identifying information.
  • Responses entered in its practice tools are processed on the student’s device and are not sent to or saved by the coursebook authors.
  • Do not enter names, diagnoses, family secrets, addresses, legal details, or other sensitive information in public practice tools.
  • Submit assignments and graded work only through Canvas or another institutionally approved system.
  • For the Family Book Project, change names and omit details that could identify the family. Obtain permission before using another person’s photograph.
  • The coursebook host, Canvas, and optional third-party media may process ordinary technical information under their own privacy policies. Embedded YouTube video is optional, uses privacy-enhanced mode, and is accompanied by explanatory course text.

Accessibility review summary

The September 2026 editorial check reviewed the coursebook’s document language, skip navigation, heading structure, image alternative text, embedded-frame titles, form labels, keyboard-usable native controls, focus visibility, responsive layout, reduced-motion support, and local handling of practice responses. The review found no missing page-language declarations, image alternative-text attributes, embedded-frame titles, or empty buttons in the published HTML set. The video embed was also changed to privacy-enhanced mode.

This was a code and editorial review, not certification by an outside accessibility auditor and not a substitute for testing with every screen reader or access technology.

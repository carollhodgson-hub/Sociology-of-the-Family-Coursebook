Welcome to the book

A coursebook with a guiding perspective

This coursebook examines family as both a personal relationship network and a social institution connected to culture, law, education, work, healthcare, inequality, and social change.

Its central question is not whether families should reproduce one fixed historical model. It is how families—and the institutions surrounding them—can help children develop safety, belonging, judgment, resilience, and the knowledge needed to enter adult roles in the society they will actually inhabit.

Guiding idea

Families help prepare the next generation for adult life. As technology, work, education, relationships, and public life change, guidance must change too. Core commitments—care, dignity, responsibility, honesty, cooperation, and nonviolence—can remain while the skills used to live those values evolve.

Student access and privacy

Accessibility, accommodations, practice activities, and privacy

Accessibility commitment. This coursebook was designed and reviewed using WCAG 2.1 Level AA and ADA Title II accessibility principles. It uses semantic headings, keyboard-operable controls, visible keyboard focus, alternative text, labeled form controls, readable sans-serif type, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support where animation appears. Accessibility is an ongoing process; an automated or editorial review cannot guarantee access for every person, device, or assistive technology.

Disability access and accommodations. If a page, image, chart, video, download, or activity creates a barrier, contact the instructor and the college Disability Services/Accessibility Office using the contact information in the official syllabus or college website. An accessible alternative or reasonable accommodation will be provided as promptly as possible and without penalty.

Practice—not grades. All sliders, quizzes, inventories, animations, planners, and other interactive activities in this coursebook are optional, ungraded practice. They are not required to earn points or demonstrate completion. The core concepts are also presented in text, tables, descriptions, explanations, and feedback. A student may skip an interaction or request an equivalent text-based activity without a grade penalty.

Privacy. The coursebook does not ask students to create an account or submit identifying information. Answers entered in its practice tools are processed on the student’s device and are not sent to or saved by the authors. Do not enter names, diagnoses, family secrets, or other sensitive information. Submit graded work only through Canvas or another institutionally approved system. The hosting service and optional third-party content may process ordinary technical data under their own privacy policies; embedded video is optional and uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode.
